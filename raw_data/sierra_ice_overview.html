1.5L TGDI Hyperion

The all-new 4-cylinder 1.5 Hyperion TDGi Engine delivers thrilling power with remarkable silence. Built on three pillars:

Hyperformance – 160 PS and 255 Nm of torque for exhilarating acceleration. Hyperquiet – Near-silent operation for a smooth, calm drive. Hypertech – Advanced innovations like Variable Geometry Turbo, 350‑bar direct injection, Dual VVT, and Intelligent Lubrication for unmatched responsiveness, efficiency, and reliability.

1.5L Kryojet Diesel

The 1.5 Kryojet CRAIL Diesel 4‑cylinder engine delivers a perfect balance of torque-rich performance and fuel efficiency, producing 118 PS and 280 Nm. Designed for strong low-end power and effortless highway cruising, it makes every drive smooth and responsive.

1.5 Revotron NA Gasoline

The all-new 1.5 Revotron NA Gasoline 4‑cylinder engine delivers 106 PS and 145 Nm, offering smooth, efficient performance with responsive acceleration, balanced fuel efficiency, and a quiet, comfortable drive.
