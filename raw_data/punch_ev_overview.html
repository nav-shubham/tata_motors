Frequently Asked Questions

The Tata Punch EV price in India starts from approximately ₹9.69 lakh*, depending on the variant and battery pack chosen. The on-road price may vary across cities based on registration charges, insurance, and applicable taxes. You can book the Tata Punch EV test drive online and check the latest offers and availability in your city.

The Tata Punch EV is available in multiple exterior colour options across its personas, including:

- Smart (Single Tone): Supernova Copper, Pure Grey, Bengal Rouge, Pristine White
- Adventure (Single Tone): Supernova Copper, Pure Grey, Bengal Rouge, Pristine White, Caramel
- Empowered (Dual Tone): Fearless Yellow, Pure Grey, Empowered Oxide, Pristine White, Bengal Rouge, Supernova Copper, Caramel

Colour availability may vary by variant and dealership stock. You can explore available colour combinations and configure your Tata Punch EV.

The Tata Punch EV is offered in multiple variants across 30 kWh and 40 kWh LFP prismatic cell battery packs, allowing customers to choose according to their budget and feature preferences. The variants include:

- Smart 30
- Smart+ 30 and 40
- Adventure 40
- Empowered 40
- Empowered+ S 40

Each variant offers different levels of technology, comfort, and safety features. The 40 kWh battery comes with a Lifetime HV Battery Warranty for the first private owner. You can explore all variants on the Tata Punch EV page.
