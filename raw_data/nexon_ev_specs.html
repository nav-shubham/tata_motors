    Frequently Asked Questions

    Tata Nexon EV is powered by a 45 kWh high-density lithium-ion battery with Gen-2 motor producing 106 kW power and 215 Nm torque. It delivers an ARAI certified range of 489 km (MIDC Part 1 + Part 2) and accelerates from 0-100 km/h in 8.9 seconds. Discover Tata Nexon EV to explore all features and variants.

    The Tata Nexon EV supports DC fast charging with a 60 kW DC charger it can go from 10%-80% charge in just 40 minutes. Home charging with 7.2 kW AC wall box achieves 10%-100% in 6 hours 36 minutes. Standard 15A socket charging takes 17 hours 36 minutes for full charge. It also features V2V (Vehicle-to-Vehicle) charging capability to charge other EVs.

    Tata Nexon EV measures 3,995 mm in length, 1,802 mm in width, and 1,625 mm in height with a 2,498 mm wheelbase and is built on a dedicated EV architecture featuring liquid-cooled IP67 rated battery pack for enhanced durability. It offers spacious cabin with ergonomic seating for 5 passengers and offers adequate boot space of 350 L for family trips.

    Tata Nexon features Bharat NCAP safety standards with ADAS (Advanced Driver Assistance System). Safety features include multiple airbags, ESP with i-VBAC, hill ascent & descent control, All Disc Brakes, Front parking sensors, TPMS, 360-degree camera with Blind spot view monitor, ISOFIX mounts, SOS Call, Auto hold, Rain Sensing wipers and Auto Head Lamps. Configure your Tata Nexon EV to explore all safety options.

    The Tata Nexon EV offers premium technology features:

     

    • 31.24 cm Cinematic Touchscreen by HARMANTM with wireless Android Auto and Apple CarPlay
    • 26.03 cm HD fully reconfigurable Digital Cockpit
    • JBL Cinematic Sound System with 9 speakers and 360° 3D surround sound
    • Multi Mood Ambient Lighting
    • V2V (Vehicle-to-Vehicle) and V2L (Vehicle-to-Load) technology
    • Arcade.ev app suite
    • iRA connected car technology
    • OTA (Over-the-Air) software updates
    • Voice-assisted panoramic sunroof
    • Multi Drive Modes (Eco, City, Sport) with regenerative braking paddle shifters
    • Wireless Charger
    • Air Purifier with AQI display 

    *Electric Drivetrain – Motor Power - *Export models are also available with 98 kW Power option in the 45kWh battery Version
